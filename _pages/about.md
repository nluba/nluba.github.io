I am an environmental social scientist with a Ph.D. in Earth System Science from Florida International University (FIU). My research focuses on climate and disaster resilience, socioeconomic vulnerability, natural resource management, and human–environment interactions.

My work integrates quantitative research, econometric methods, and geospatial analysis to investigate how individuals, households, and communities prepare for, respond to, and recover from environmental hazards. I am particularly interested in understanding the socioeconomic dimensions of disaster risk, identifying disparities in vulnerability and recovery, and generating evidence to support informed policy and resilience planning.

My interdisciplinary background spans environmental science, natural resource management, urban and regional planning, and geographic information systems (GIS).
